## Description

GetNit is the fastest way to declutter your iPhone photo gallery. Just swipe — right to keep, left to delete. It's like Tinder, but for your photos.

We all have thousands of photos taking up space — screenshots we forgot about, duplicates, blurry shots, old memes. Cleaning them up one by one in the Photos app is tedious. GetNit makes it effortless.

**How it works:**
1. Swipe through your photos one by one. Swipe right to keep, left to mark for deletion.
2. When you're done, review everything you marked in a grid. Tap to un-mark any you want to keep.
3. Hit delete and all marked photos are removed in one batch. Free up space instantly.

**Features:**
• Tinder-style swipe gestures — right to keep, left to delete
• Batch delete — nothing is deleted until you confirm at the end
• Undo any swipe — made a mistake? One tap to go back
• Review grid — see all marked photos before deleting, tap to un-mark
• Smart filters — filter by album, screenshots only, or date range
• Remembers your progress — already-reviewed photos won't reappear
• Onboarding screen — guides you through the swipe flow on first launch
• 100% private — no servers, no cloud, no tracking, no ads

**Privacy first:**
GetNit works entirely on-device. Your photos never leave your phone. No cloud uploads, no analytics, no data collection. We literally cannot see your photos. All deletions go through the standard iOS PhotoKit framework.

Take control of your photo gallery today. Get GetNit.
